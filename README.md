# 🧬 Project: Lifeform — A Creature That Evolves From Your Real Life

## 🎯 Concept

**“A lifeform that evolves based on how you live in the real world.”**

Lifeform is a mobile experience where a single digital creature grows, mutates, and evolves not from in-app interaction, but from **your real-world behavior**:

- How much you move  
- Where you go  
- Who you pass by  
- How often you stay off your phone  

In a shrinking, increasingly isolated society, Lifeform reintroduces value to **physical presence, weak social connections, and everyday movement**.

---

## 🌏 Motivation

### 1. Shrinking Japan → Fewer Encounters

- Population decline → fewer spontaneous interactions  
- Urban isolation → weaker social fabric  
- Rural depopulation → physical disconnection  

👉 **Chance encounters are disappearing**

---

### 2. Smartphone / SNS Addiction

- Endless scrolling replaces real-world engagement  
- Social interaction becomes algorithmically mediated  
- Attention is captured, not consciously directed  

👉 **Digital behavior crowds out physical life**

---

### 3. Loss of “Meaningless but Valuable” Experiences

- No friction → no serendipity  
- Efficiency → loss of randomness  
- Strong ties dominate, weak ties vanish  

👉 **We’ve lost casual, low-stakes human presence**

---

## 🧠 Core Idea

> **What if your creature only grows when you live more in the real world—and less on your phone?**

Lifeform inverts traditional engagement design:

- **Less screen time → more growth**
- **More movement → more evolution**
- **More real-world encounters → more diversity**

---

## ⚙️ Core System

### 🧬 One Lifeform

- Each user has a **single evolving creature**
- No collection, no replacement
- Your creature is a **reflection of your life patterns**

---

### 📊 Four Core Traits (Internal State)

All real-world inputs are mapped into four latent traits:

| Trait        | Meaning                         |
|--------------|---------------------------------|
| Physical     | Movement, activity              |
| Social       | Encounters with others          |
| Exploration  | Novelty of places and behavior  |
| Digital      | Phone / SNS usage               |

These are not shown directly — they manifest visually.

---

## 🔄 Core Features

### 🚶 Physical Activity Tracking

- Step count (daily movement)
- Activity intensity (e.g., gym visits inferred from dwell time)

**Effect:**
- Higher Physical → stronger, larger, more energetic forms

---

### 📍 Location & Exploration

- Detects **new vs repeated locations**
- Tracks diversity of movement patterns

**Effect:**
- High Exploration → adaptive, fluid, diverse forms  
- Low Exploration → static, rooted, repetitive forms  

---

### 📡 Bluetooth “Passing Encounters”

- Detects nearby users via proximity (e.g., BLE)
- Focus on **anonymous, low-friction interactions**

**Effect:**
- New encounters → mutation / diversity  
- Repeated encounters → stabilization  

👉 Encourages **weak ties**, not deep social graphs

---

### 📵 Screen Time / SNS Usage

- Integrates with device screen-time APIs
- Tracks duration and timing (e.g., late-night usage)

**Effect:**
- High usage → growth suppression / corruption  
- Low usage → unlocks evolution paths  

👉 **Inverse reward system**: less engagement = more progress

---

### 🗺️ Environment-Aware Evolution

Location is abstracted into environmental context:

- Urban vs rural
- High vs low population density
- Routine vs novel environments

**Effect:**
- Dense environments → socially adaptive forms  
- Sparse environments → rare, self-sufficient evolutions  

👉 **Shrinking regions become high-value zones**

---

## 🧬 Evolution Design

### 1. Continuous Evolution (Not Discrete)

- No fixed evolution tree
- Creature form emerges from trait combinations

👉 Infinite variation — no two creatures are identical

---

### 2. Life History Imprint

> “How you lived” is embedded into the creature

Examples:

- Night activity → nocturnal features  
- Repetitive routines → patterned morphology  
- High mobility → lightweight / agile forms  

---

### 3. Serendipity & Mutation

Rare evolution triggers:

- First-time location + new encounter  
- Low-density areas  
- Behavior deviations from normal patterns  

👉 Designed randomness reflects **real-world unpredictability**

---

## 🤖 Role of AI (Invisible Layer)

AI is not user-facing. It acts as:

- **Behavior interpreter** (pattern recognition)
- **Evolution modulator** (personalized variation)
- **Serendipity engine** (dynamic rarity tuning)

👉 Ensures:
- No two users converge to the same outcomes  
- Subtle adaptation without explicit rules  

---

## 🧩 Experience Principles

### 1. Low Interaction

- Minimal notifications  
- No infinite scroll  
- No active grinding  

👉 The app should not compete for attention

---

### 2. Interpretive UI

- No stats dashboards  
- Users infer behavior from visual changes  

👉 Encourages curiosity, not optimization

---

### 3. Real Life > App Time

- The best way to “play” is to **not open the app**

---

## 🏁 MVP Scope

### Required

- Single lifeform
- Step tracking
- Basic location detection (new vs repeated)
- Screen time integration
- Simple evolution mapping

### Optional

- Bluetooth encounter detection
- Environmental classification (urban/rural)
- Basic mutation triggers

---

## 🧭 Positioning

Lifeform is not:

- A game (no grinding loops)
- A social network (no posting/likes)
- A productivity tool (no explicit goals)

It is:

> **A passive system that reflects your lifestyle back to you, and gently nudges you toward the real world.**

---

**“A creature that evolves from your real-world behavior—and punishes you for staying on your phone.”**
